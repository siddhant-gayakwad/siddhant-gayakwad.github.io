## About

I got into DevOps the self-directed way — certifications, internships, and a lot of broken
Kubernetes clusters at 11pm — rather than through a computer-science-first path. That path
taught me to actually understand *why* a pipeline fails, not just how to re-run it.

At Techanek Technologies I work across CI/CD automation, containerization, AWS infrastructure,
and production troubleshooting. Outside work, I volunteer with the AWS Ahmedabad Community and
have spoken on stage at community events, including AWS Community Day Ahmedabad 2026.
